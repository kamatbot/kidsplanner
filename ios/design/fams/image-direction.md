# Fams visual direction

Current proposed direction: landscape-middle-ground.png. Generated with the built-in image tool; the tool does not expose an Images 2.5 model selector. This is a visual concept, not an app screenshot. Sample balances and progress drawn by the image model are illustrative and must not replace server-calculated values.

The user rejected the earlier mascot-heavy direction as too young and the sculptural/chrome direction as too corporate. The current direction uses tactile editorial illustrations, violet/coral, a learning trail, and teen-appropriate language. No generated art is integrated into the native runtime yet; the native implementation currently has the earlier code-drawn visuals. Keep native controls accessible and all balances, progress, and completion states driven by live data when aligning the implementation.

## Generation prompt

Use case: ui-mockup. Design ONE beautiful native iPad LANDSCAPE FamETC Fams screen. Audience 11–17-year-olds. Find the MIDDLE GROUND between a cute children's game and a corporate fintech dashboard. It must feel fun, warm, creative, visually rich and useful to teenagers. NOT childish, NOT corporate.
CURRENT palette strictly anchors the design: violet #6F43D6, coral #F0704F, warm greige #F1EFEC, white, dark ink #211E1B; small warm yellow accents are welcome. One consistent Space Grotesk-like geometric sans font. Moderate confident headings, not bubbly nursery type or oversized corporate typography.
Art direction: tasteful contemporary illustrated stickers with matte tactile shapes, a little paper texture and hand-drawn energy. Charming illustrated objects, subtle asymmetry, bold color blocks, occasional short motion lines. No characters or faces on objects. No chrome, marble plinths, photorealistic metallic coins, glass office sculptures, corporate chart aesthetic, fairy-tale landscapes or cartoon forests. Avoid excessive doodles and motivational slogans. Think an excellent creative app for teens that happens to teach money skills.
Flat front-on usable product screenshot, 4:3 landscape, no device frame, no collage. Native top bar back arrow, title "Fams", small circular initials avatar. Compact, polished touch controls; use the full landscape width, keep useful information above fold.
Left third: vivid violet balance card with "680 fams", "1 fam = ฿1". A small expressive illustration of a few matte violet/coral/yellow reward tokens marked with a simple F, no face, with small motion accents. Weekly progress "180 / 300 this week" with a satisfying thick progress track and subtle 100, 200, 300 markers. Below: warm lavender savings card "My next goal", a lively illustrated skateboard in coral/violet beside the actual goal info "Skateboard", "220 fams to go", progress and "Edit goal". Below: compact coral-accented "Earn extra" with a small illustrated clipboard/books, "Tidy my bookshelf", "+30 fams", "Mark done".
Right two thirds: "Your money trail", a simple subheading "Learn a little. Try it in real life." A beautifully arranged, easy-to-understand learning trail across two compact rows, connected by a confident flowing lavender path. FIVE distinct illustrated lesson objects, in this mature sticker style: a savings envelope with tokens; a shopping basket holding headphones and a trainer; a planner with colorful tabs; a growing stack of matte blocks with a small sprout as a metaphor for compounding; branching colored tiles for investing. Labels "Saving", "Needs & wants", "Planning", "Compounding", "Investing". Real buttons and completion marks, two completed, one current. The current lesson is prominently highlighted with a violet "Continue" button. No fake XP or leaderboards. There is enough illustration to make the screen joyful, but objects never compete with text and controls.
Lower-right compact "Money lab" entry, "Explore how savings grow", with a fun coral curved arrow and violet bars drawn as a small editorial illustration, not a corporate 3D chart.
Use expressive native-feeling controls, precise spacing, tasteful layered color and restrained shadows. The result should appeal to both a 12-year-old and a 16-year-old: playful without babying them, polished without feeling like a bank.



## Native implementation
The app uses the six-object `FamsArtwork` atlas in Assets.xcassets, generated from this direction on 2026-09-12. The atlas deliberately has a solid warm paper background; six tiles are sliced once in native code. No screenshot text or balances are baked into the app. Native controls render live wallet, goal, chore and lesson state. The phone layout is compact; landscape iPad places the wallet, goal and chores beside a three-column topic trail. All lessons remain individually available. Money Lab uses native controls and the existing monthly projection calculation, without an embedded login or web view.
