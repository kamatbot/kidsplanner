# Product

## Register

product

## Users

FamETC is used by parents and kids in the middle of real family logistics: a
parent coordinating the week from a phone, a kid checking what is due next, a
family making decisions in chat, and adults planning meals or travel together.
Parents are the customer and organizer; kids are role-scoped participants and
daily users. The initial wedge is families connected to St Andrews, with a
broader family audience over time.

The primary job is to remove the remembering, re-entering, chasing, and
coordination work from family life. The product should answer "what matters
next?", turn messages and external information into shared actions, and keep
the family moving without requiring everyone to learn a project-management
tool.

## Product Purpose

FamETC is the family time saver: one trusted hub for the family's schedule,
schoolwork, meals, trips, activities, and communication. It should start
saving time during onboarding by importing or connecting useful information,
then continue saving time through a unified Today view, automatic reminders,
shared ownership, and reusable family data.

Success means the family opens FamETC to know what needs attention, spends less
time asking the same questions or rebuilding plans in different apps, and can
complete common weekly coordination jobs faster than before. The product's
North Star is **family coordination time saved per week**, supported by
activation, completion, and retention measures.

## Brand Personality

Efficient, reassuring, and family-friendly. FamETC should feel capable and
quietly helpful: it reduces work, makes the next step obvious, and keeps the
family informed without becoming noisy, childish, or corporate.

## Anti-references

- A generic calendar where the family still has to interpret what matters.
- A collection of disconnected feature pages that each maintain their own
  people, dates, lists, and notifications.
- A blank dashboard that asks a busy parent to configure everything before it
  becomes useful.
- A chat app that stores conversation but leaves the family to turn messages
  into tasks, events, shopping items, or decisions manually.
- A project-management system that makes kids feel like employees and parents
  feel like administrators.

## Design Principles

1. **Save time before asking for time.** Import, infer, and prefill from school
   feeds, messages, receipts, bookings, and existing family data; ask for
   confirmation only where trust or safety requires it.
2. **One family timeline, many useful views.** Events, homework, meals, trips,
   activities, and decisions should share canonical people, dates, ownership,
   and notification behavior. Views can specialize; data should not fragment.
3. **Make the next action obvious.** Today is an action queue, not a widget
   wall. Prioritize urgency, dependencies, preparation, and ownership over
   decorative content.
4. **Conversation becomes coordination.** Chat is an input surface for the
   family system. Important messages should become structured, searchable,
   assignable objects without forcing people to repeat themselves elsewhere.
5. **Parents orchestrate; kids participate.** Give parents oversight and
   control while giving kids simple, satisfying actions they can own. Respect
   role boundaries and minimize kid data.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Use WCAG 2.2 AA as the working baseline. Support Dynamic Type and size-class
adaptation on iOS, keyboard and screen-reader navigation on the web, visible
focus states, minimum touch targets, semantic status text in addition to color,
reduced motion, and clear plain-language copy. Design for a parent using one
hand while distracted and a kid using a larger touch device; do not rely on
color, hover, or tiny icon-only controls to communicate meaning.
