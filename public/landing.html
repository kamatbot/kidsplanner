Keep the moving parts together.

Small moments of coordination, in the same place as the school week.

  • Family chat

    One thread per family, plus quick pings for pickups and kit. Kids can read and reply safely.

  • Family planning

    Plan meals, trips and the moving parts of family life together in one shared place.

  • Quizzes

    Short rounds that follow this term's subjects — five minutes before dinner counts.

  • Puzzles

    A daily puzzle for each age band. Siblings can race; parents usually lose.

Keep everyone pointed at next.

One shared view for what is due, where it happens, and who needs a nudge.

  • Shared calendar

    School events and family plans in one week view, filtered per child.

  • Goals & habits

    Reading, practice, bedtime — small habits tracked without nagging.

  • Homework hub

    Due today, due soon, overdue — plus a photo of the diary page when Moodle is thin.

  • Reminders

    Kit bags, permission slips, deadlines — nudges that arrive the night before, not after.

Questions parents ask first

Is Fam ETC really free?

Yes. Fam ETC is free for STA parents, with no card required. You need an STA invite code to sign up. See pricing.

Is this an official STA app?

No — Fam ETC is built by parents for STA families and reads the Moodle you already use.

Who can see our data?

Only your family. Moodle access is parent-only and read-only; Fam ETC never posts as you. Read our privacy policy.

What if a teacher doesn't use Moodle?

Snap the homework diary page — Fam ETC reads it and adds the tasks alongside the synced ones.
