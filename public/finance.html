    What if you kept saving?

    Explore how regular saving and compound growth could add up. These are imaginary results, not promised returns. The model compounds the yearly rate monthly, adds savings at each month’s end, and excludes fees, taxes and inflation.

    Three things a calculator can’t promise

    Risk: investments can lose value. Growth is not a fixed reward.

    Inflation: prices can rise, so the same baht may buy less later.

    Diversification: spreading money across different investments helps avoid putting everything in one basket. It cannot remove all risk.
