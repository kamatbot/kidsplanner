What it does

Getting started

Set up in one evening

Three steps. The middle one takes the longest — that's normal.

Step 01

Create your family

One passkey account, add each child. About a minute. Your kids can't sign up on their own — you create every child profile and approve every child device.

Step 02

Connect the school

In the app, go to Settings → School links. For each child, paste the homework link and the timetable link from the school system — the same links already in your browser or email from St Andrews. You paste your own links; Fam ETC only ever reads them, encrypted, and checks the school every eight hours from then on. Nothing to retype after that.

Look in: Settings → School links
Step 03

Open Today

Homework, ECAs, the timetable and points are already there. Say hi in the family thread.

Privacy & safety

You're handing us your children's week. Here's the deal.

Plain commitments, not a policy summary.

  • Encrypted at restChat messages and every piece of children's data are encrypted on our servers.
  • School sync is read-onlyThe school link is encrypted and never shown again after you paste it. We read; we never write back. Checked every eight hours.
  • No ads, aggregate analyticsWe count daily totals, not people. No per-person analytics profile for you or your kids.
  • Kids can't sign up aloneA parent creates every child profile and approves every child device.
  • Parents keep the keysAny parent can delete any message in the family and remove any member.

Full policy at fametc.com/privacy.
